Use case: product-mockup / ads-marketing.
Create ONE exceptionally polished, photorealistic product illustration for the Maple website, landscape 3:2 composition, high resolution. Maple is a quiet iPad notebook where a human and an AI think together directly in handwritten ink. The visual should make a designer want to pick up a pencil.

Scene: an almost top-down, very gently angled product photograph of a thin dark iPad in LANDSCAPE orientation on a warm ivory surface. The iPad occupies roughly 90% of the image width and 85% of its height. Show the entire device, its thin natural bezel and realistic soft contact shadow. A single natural human hand holding a white Apple Pencil enters from the lower right and is adding a small graphite line on the page. No other props or people. No dramatic lighting, glossy reflections or decorative room background. Soft diffuse daylight, restrained editorial product photography.

Screen: a large warm off-white unruled notebook page, FILLED with a beautiful coherent design study of a small reading corner. It should feel actively thought through by hand, not a template, presentation slide or polished architectural deliverable. Use the screen area generously: around 70% interesting handwritten content and 30% breathing space. Fine graphite human notes and exploratory sketches are visibly joined by fine ink-blue Maple contributions, with a small amount of muted coral editing markup.

Page content: handwritten title "The reading corner" toward the upper left. An informal annotated floor plan at the left, a larger elegant perspective sketch of a cozy chair, small bookshelf, standing reading lamp and window toward the middle-right, two little chair or lamp variations along the bottom, a small illumination diagram with rays and arrows in the upper right, a few tiny olive, ochre and terracotta material swatches beside short notes. A human graphite question "softer light ?" is answered immediately beside it in blue handwriting "Bounce light off the wall". Other sparse short handwritten notes: "keep the window clear", "warm oak", "linen", "try a lower shelf". One hand-drawn rectangular box contains a blue alternative lamp sketch. One coral circle around a lampshade with a small arrow signals an edit. Connect related thoughts with a few graceful hand-drawn arrows. The collaboration is visible by how the blue notes and diagrams build directly on the graphite work, sharing the same page.

Ink should have authentic pressure variation, organic handwritten lettering, a few searching construction lines and loose crossings-out, balanced with beautifully legible penmanship. Fine marks rather than heavy cartoon outlines. Blue additions should look like ink on the same paper, not overlays or chat bubbles. Keep all diagram content small enough to form a rich complete page but large enough to appreciate. Avoid long paragraphs and gibberish text.

Minimal plausible notebook chrome: a tiny "Pages" control at the screen's top left, a discreet little pencil-and-eraser tool dock at the top center, a small "Maple" control at the top right. These controls must remain peripheral and quiet. No large navigation, sidebars, chat panels or logos. Do not reproduce the marketing website on the iPad. The device displays the illustrated notebook workspace only. No floating AI robots, sparkle icons, neon, giant headlines, photographic collage, extra tablets, watermarks, marketing captions or text outside the screen.

Palette matches Maple: warm paper, charcoal graphite, rich muted blue ink, restrained coral edit marks, soft olive and ochre accents. A calm and premium image with natural realism and the charm of an actual working sketchbook.
