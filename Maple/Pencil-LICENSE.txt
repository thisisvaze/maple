PencilSynth.swift synthesises the pencil's sound as it's drawn: a scraping model in which
the nib reads a paper-tooth profile laid out along the stroke (five scales, 1.2 to 0.025
points), so grain pitch and density follow pen speed; graphite crackle at about two
fibres per point; a 420 Hz sheet resonance; and a speed-tracking low-pass. It contains no
recordings and needs no runtime downloads. checks/pencil-check.swift measures it.

The eraser and edit brush reuse the same motion model with distinct contact scales,
filters and fibre levels: a broad rubber rub and a finer felt swish. The original
pencil parameters are unchanged. All three fade with speed, pressure and pen lift.

The approach follows Skuzic's touch engine (src/audio/touch.ts and
ios/Skuzic/Audio/TouchEngine.swift) by Aaditya Vaze, whose grain also speeds up with the
pen; PencilSound.swift keeps its 40 ms motion smoothing and 60 ms still-pen silence.
