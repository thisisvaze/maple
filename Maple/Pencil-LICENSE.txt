pencil.wav is a generated paper-friction texture based on Skuzic's
src/audio/touch.ts and ios/Skuzic/Audio/TouchEngine.swift by Aaditya Vaze.
Motion smoothing in PencilSound.swift uses the same 40 ms time constant.

Generated offline by tools/render-pencil.py: 140 Hz paper grain, roughness
0.85, 4.2 kHz low-pass, 700 Hz high-pass and a 5 dB peak at 3.2 kHz.
Four seconds of mono 24 kHz PCM, with an equal-power seamless wrap.
It contains no recorded strokes or pen lifts, and needs no runtime downloads.
